    Each bar is the number of contracts in that DTE bucket that pass the other four criteria. A longer bar means a deeper market: more strikes you can enter and exit close to mid.

    Bid-ask spread

    Median
    Tightest
    Widest

    The spread is the ask minus the bid, shown as a percentage of the mid price, across the contracts that pass. It is the transaction cost of a round trip.

    Tight (under about 2%): an actively quoted contract; fills land near mid and slippage is small.

    Wide (above 5%): a thin market. A $2.00 option with a 10% spread gives up about $20 per contract on a round trip, before the underlying moves at all.

The liquidity gate

The liquidity gate is a pre-trade screen. Before any research is committed to an underlying, it establishes whether that underlying's listed options can be traded at an acceptable execution cost. Where they cannot, the request is halted unless the user overrides the result.

Execution cost: the bid-ask spread

Every listed option is quoted two-sided. The bid is the highest price a buyer is currently willing to pay. The ask, or offer, is the lowest price a seller will accept. A market order buys at the ask and sells at the bid, so the spread between them is a transaction cost incurred on every round trip, independent of any view on the underlying.

Spreads are not uniform across the chain. Relative to premium, low-priced contracts are materially more expensive to trade than higher-priced ones.

Why the screen runs first

  • Negligible cost. Retrieving a single option chain takes seconds. Researching an underlying with several AI agents takes minutes and incurs model costs.
  • Early rejection. Where the options cannot be traded at an acceptable cost, there is no case for developing a thesis.
  • A bounded opportunity set. Later stages select only from contracts that cleared the screen.

Screening procedure

  1. Retrieve the option chainFrom the live feed during the regular session; from the most recent daily snapshot outside it.
  2. Reject invalid quotesA contract with no bid, no ask, or a crossed market (bid above ask) fails unconditionally. Non-standard or adjusted series are flagged and excluded.
  3. Compute per-contract measuresQuoted spread as a percentage of mid, days to expiration (DTE), and moneyness, measured as the distance from strike to spot.
  4. Apply the five screening controlsEach contract is tested against each control independently.
  5. Determine eligibilityAn underlying is eligible when at least two contracts clear every control. A single qualifying contract does not constitute a tradeable market.
  6. Report the outcomePass or fail, the number of eligible contracts and, on a fail, the binding control. A fail may be overridden by the user.
  7. Write the decision recordThe outcome, the data source and its timestamp, the control settings in force, the eligible count, and any override with its stated rationale.

Stage output: the screening summary

The gate does not return the full list of eligible contracts. A liquid underlying can clear several hundred, and without a thesis there is no basis for choosing among them. The user is shown a one-screen summary and decides whether to proceed.

The 103-name sample

Counts are expressed out of 103 because the coverage universe held 103 underlyings on the observation date, 13 November 2026.

  • A curated starter universe. It comprises 7 index and sector ETFs (including SPY, QQQ and IWM) and 96 large-capitalisation US equities with actively traded options, diversified across technology, financials, healthcare, energy, consumer and industrials. It was selected by hand and follows no index methodology.
  • Now 106 names. S&P 500 index options (SPX), Mini-SPX (XSP) and the 20+ year Treasury bond ETF (TLT) were added on 15 November.
  • It does not restrict what can be screened. The universe determines only which underlyings the daily snapshot records. During the regular session the gate screens any optionable underlying against the live chain. The universe is relevant only outside market hours and for displaying an underlying's screening history.
  • Selection bias. These are among the most liquid names in the market. A broader universe would show a lower proportion of eligible underlyings.

    • A live result takes precedence over history. An underlying that was eligible for a month and fails today is treated as failing. History indicates only whether today's result is typical.
    • A fail halts the request by default, with the binding control stated. The user may override it.

    System-proposed and user-specified contracts

    System-proposed contracts

    The controls are binding. The system never proposes a contract outside the current settings, nor one with fewer than 7 days to expiration.

    User-specified contracts

    A contract specified by the user may fall outside any control. The system reports the execution cost and writes the override to the decision record. Invalid quotes still fail.

    Discretionary override

    The gate is advisory, and the decision rests with the user. Where an underlying or a contract fails the screen, the user may override the result and advance the underlying, or selected contracts, to the next stage.

    • Scope. Any of the five controls, the two-contract minimum, and a provisional or unavailable result outside market hours.
    • Process. The system reports the execution cost of the failing contract, requires a one-line rationale, and records the override.
    • Unchanged. The system does not propose a failing contract of its own accord. An override advances only contracts the user has selected.
    • Exception. An invalid quote, with no bid or no ask, cannot be advanced because it carries no price.

    Decision records and audit trail

    Every decision at every stage of the workflow generates a decision record at the time it is made. The gate's record is the first in the chain. Where a contract is ultimately traded, the complete chain is retained permanently in the system's memory and linked to that trade.

    Pre-trade re-validation

    The screen is applied a second time once a thesis exists and specific contracts have been selected. That pass always runs against the live chain, on the exact contracts, immediately before position sizing and order ticket generation. The first pass determines whether an underlying merits research. The second confirms the contracts to be traded.

    Data and limitations. All charts on this page derive from a single intraday snapshot taken on 13 November 2026, covering the 103 underlyings in the coverage universe on that date. A broader universe would show a lower proportion of eligible underlyings. The daily job now records these counts each trading day, and the ranges should be recalibrated once several weeks of observations are available.
